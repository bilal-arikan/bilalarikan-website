YoutubeGlance

Turn YouTube captions into a summary prompt for your preferred AI assistant. Choose a language and summary style, then start directly from the video player.

16 March 2026AIChrome ExtensionTypeScript
Chrome Web Store View source

Get the key ideas from a long video with a caption-based workflow. YoutubeGlance extracts available YouTube captions and prepares a prompt for the AI assistant you choose.

From video to summary prompt

  1. Open a YouTube video with available captions and select Glance in the player.
  2. Choose your summary language, AI service, and prompt template.
  3. Insert the prepared prompt into the selected AI website, or copy it for manual use.

Your preferred AI, your summary style

Choose from short, detailed, web-enhanced, or custom prompt templates. Supported destinations include ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Studio, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi, and MiniMax.

Automatic submission is optional. The read-aloud option can activate a supported AI website's own speech control. Available features depend on the selected service and your account.

How your data is used

Preferences and custom templates are stored locally in your browser. When you transfer a prompt, the video title, captions, and any custom instructions included in it are shared with the AI website you selected. That service handles the content under its own policies.

YoutubeGlance prepares prompts; the selected AI service generates the summary. Caption availability and quality affect the result, and a service may require sign-in or its own subscription.

Built with TypeScript for Chrome. Explore the source on GitHub.
